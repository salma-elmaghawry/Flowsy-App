Flowsy Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Flowsy ("the app") helps you split your money into wallets and plan what you will spend. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. The app is developed by Salma Elmaghawry.

Data we collect

  • Account data. Your email address and password, used only to create your account and sign you in. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.
  • Financial data you enter. Wallet names, colors, balances, planned spending, notes, and transactions. We store this so you can see it on any device you sign in to.
  • Settings on your device. Language, theme, and app-lock preference are saved only on your phone.

Biometric data

If you turn on app lock, your fingerprint or face check is performed entirely by Android. The app never receives, stores, or transmits biometric data.

How we use and store data

Your data is used only to provide the app's features. It is stored on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) servers and encrypted in transit. Security rules ensure only your signed-in account can read or change your data.

We do not sell your data, show ads, use analytics or tracking, or share your data with third parties. Google processes the data only as our infrastructure provider.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all your data at any time from Settings › Delete account in the app. Deletion is immediate and permanent. You can also request deletion by email, see how to delete your account.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
