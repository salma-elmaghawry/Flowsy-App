Delete your Flowsy account

Flowsy, by Salma Elmaghawry

From the app

  1. Open Flowsy and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your password and tap Delete permanently.

By email

If you can no longer open the app, email salmaelmaghawry91@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Flowsy account deletion". Your account is deleted within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your account, email address, and every wallet, planned spending item, note, and transaction are permanently deleted. Nothing is kept.

أو ابعت إيميل من نفس البريد المسجل إلى salmaelmaghawry91@gmail.com وسيتم حذف الحساب خلال 30 يوماً.
